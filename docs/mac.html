Save from your Mac

Evening Reader Saver puts a button in your browser's toolbar that saves the page you're reading into your Evening Reader library in iCloud. It reads the article from the page in front of you, so it saves what you can see, including sites you're signed in to. The article reaches your iPhone and iPad ready to read, offline too.

Download Evening Reader Saver

For macOS 14 or later. Your library needs iCloud Drive turned on for Evening Reader on your iPhone or iPad.

Setting it up

1. Open it once
Move Evening Reader Saver to your Applications folder and open it. Opening it sets up Safari, and Chrome and the other Chromium browsers on this Mac, all at once. There's nothing else to install, and it doesn't need to stay open.
2. In Safari
Safari › Settings › Extensions, then turn on Save to Evening Reader and allow it on all websites, so the button works on any page.
2. In Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi or Arc
Add Save to Evening Reader from the Chrome Web Store. If a browser was installed after you opened Evening Reader Saver, open it again.

Saving

How do I save a page?
Click the button, press ⌥⇧E, or right-click and choose Save Page to Evening Reader. To save a link without opening it, right-click it and choose Save Link to Evening Reader; the app fetches it the next time it opens.
It says the helper isn't installed.
Open Evening Reader Saver again. That happens if it was moved after it was first opened, or if it was opened straight from Downloads before it was moved.
