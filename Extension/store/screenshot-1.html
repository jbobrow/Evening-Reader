On Reading by Lamplight

By Ada Marlow · 9 min read

There is a kind of reading that only happens after dark. The day's work is done, the house has gone quiet, and a single lamp makes a small warm room inside the larger one.

A page read this way asks for nothing back. No one is waiting on an answer; there is nothing to click and nowhere else to be. It is the slowest reading we do, and very often the best.
